      About
      
          
      Unofficial, not affiliated with AJAZZ. Works in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer
            (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS) over the USB cable or the 2.4G dongle. Phones, Safari and
            Firefox don't support WebHID, and the keyboard doesn't expose its settings over Bluetooth.
      
          
      On Linux, allow your user to open the keyboard once:
            curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shrey715/ak820ctl/main/udev/70-ajazz-ak820.rules | sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/70-ajazz-ak820.rules && sudo udevadm control --reload && sudo udevadm trigger
      
          
      The keyboard can't report its settings back, so this page remembers what it last sent, in your browser.
            Prefer the terminal? ak820ctl has a CLI, a TUI and an AppImage.
